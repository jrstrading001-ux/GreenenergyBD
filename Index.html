How Solar Net Metering Works

Net metering allows solar energy system owners to feed surplus electricity into the grid and offset their utility bills.

1

Solar Power Generation

Rooftop solar panels absorb sunlight and convert it into DC power, which grid-tied inverters change into AC electricity for home or facility use.

2

Grid Export Measurement

When solar generation exceeds local consumption, the extra electricity flows back into the grid through our bidirectional net meter.

3

Net Billing & Savings

Your utility utility calculates the net difference between Imported and Exported kWh units, drastically reducing monthly power bills.
